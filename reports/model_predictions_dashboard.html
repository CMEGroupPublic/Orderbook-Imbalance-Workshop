Microstructural Saliency & Alpha Generalization Takeaways

1. The Cost of Signal Smoothing

Exponential Moving Average (EMA) filters are highly effective for removing short-term quote flickering noise. However, they introduce a mathematical group delay of approximately 9.5 seconds (for a 20-period span). For an ultra-short 10-minute return prediction, this phase lag degrades signal timeliness, meaning un-smoothed, instantaneous imbalance features contain substantially higher predictive alpha.

2. Generalization Under High Regularization

By constraining maximum tree depth to 3 and applying heavy weight penalties (L1 Lasso of 8.0 and L2 Ridge of 15.0), we successfully insulated the XGBoost models from overfitting on granular clock time. This forces the model to ignore microsecond noise and focus entirely on structural regimes, resolving out-of-sample trend divergences.
